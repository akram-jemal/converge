# Converge

*A personal space for everything network engineering.*

Converge is where I document what I learn, build, experiment with, and figure out along the way in network engineering.

The idea behind this repository is simple: I want a place to put my knowledge into practice, explore networking concepts beyond the theory, and share what I learn with anyone who might find it useful.

Over time, I'll be adding different kinds of content, from hands-on exercises and troubleshooting scenarios to technical notes, configurations, network designs, and anything else worth documenting.

I'm not following a particular certification roadmap or sticking to one platform or technology. The goal is to keep learning, challenge myself with increasingly complex topics, and build a collection of resources that grows alongside my experience.

Everything here is independently created and maintained by me.

## What's Here?

- **Labs** — Practical exercises and scenarios to explore networking technologies.
- **Investigations** — Troubleshooting, problem-solving, and understanding why things work (or don't).
- **Designs** — Network topologies, configurations, and design ideas.
- **Notes & Guides** — Concepts, explanations, and things I've learned along the way.
- **Tools** — Scripts, automation, and other useful networking projects.

The repository will continue to evolve as I explore new areas of networking.
